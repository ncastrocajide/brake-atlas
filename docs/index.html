brake-atlas

Where each driver brakes, corner by corner, in qualifying and races. Pick a track, then a corner on the map or in the table.

All corners

One brake point is only known to about ±10 m (the timing feed is 4 Hz), so single cells are noisy; the patterns across a row or a column are what to read. Hatched cells have no clean data. Data: OpenF1. Corner positions: MultiViewer. Method and findings in the repository.
